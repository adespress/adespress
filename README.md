🌍 Pinball: Climate Change Emergency
"Our Mother Earth is on the edge. Restore planetary equilibrium before time runs out."
Pinball: Climate Change Emergency is a fast-paced, high-fidelity retro-arcade pinball simulation running entirely in the browser. Players navigate ecological tipping points, steer clear of industrial carbon culprits, and unlock regenerative clean technologies to cool planetary temperature anomalies and avert thermal runaway.

🎮 Core Objectives & Rules
The 5-Minute Doomsday Clock: You start in Crisis Mode with 2,500 Biosphere Points and a 5-minute () countdown clock.
The Target: Reach 30,000 Biosphere Points before the clock hits zero to avert planetary tipping points and unlock unlimited Super Harmonious mode.
Climate Collapse Threshold: In Crisis Mode, hazardous impacts deduct points and heat the Earth. If your score drops to -500 PTS, planetary carbon sinks collapse and the game ends immediately.

🔄 Two Dynamic Playfield Modes
The table physically changes its bumpers, targets, geometry, and ambient soundscapes between two operational states:
1. Deniability Zone (Crisis Mode)
Visuals & Atmosphere: High-heat CRT neon aesthetics, smoking charred snags, smoldering tree stumps, and rising ember particles.
Culprit Hazards (Point Penalties & Warming):
Bumpers: Big Oil (-220 pts), Coal Power (-180 pts), Mass Aviation (-180 pts).
Drop Targets: LNG Exports (-150 pts), Greenwashing Megaphones (-150 pts), Deforestation Chainsaws (-160 pts), Factory Farms (-140 pts).
Slingshots: Hydraulic Fracking (-120 pts), Petrochemical Plastics (-120 pts).

Emergency Lifelines:
⚡ Geo-Tech Invest Nodes: Strike the dual lightning nodes for instant speed boosts, temporary score rebounds (+300 pts), and atmospheric cooling.
The Central Portal: Shoot the ball into the central swirling portal to transition the table into Harmonic Mode.
3. Harmony Zone (Harmonic Mode)
Visuals & Atmosphere: Luminous emerald playfield, living multi-canopy climax rainforest, floating seed motes, singing birds, and honeybees.
Regenerative Solutions (Points & Cooling):
Bumpers: Regenerative Agriculture / Agroforestry (+300 pts), Solar (+260 pts), Wind Turbines (+240 pts).
Targets: Sufficiency & Degrowth (+250 pts), Cleaner Ocean (+250 pts, with splashing marine life), Circularity (+220 pts), Permaculture (+220 pts).
Slingshots: Organic Lifestyle (+60 pts), Biodiversity Meadow (+120 pts).
Crisis Sink Hazard: The central vortex contracts into a red hazard zone marked KEEP OUT. Falling back in triggers a Deniability Surge, reverting the table back to Crisis Mode.
Biosphere Resilience Shield: Above 10,000 points, every 500 points builds resilience buffering. In Harmonic mode, ball drops into the bottom basin have a scaled percentage chance to cleanly rebound without mode regression or point loss.

🔊 Sound Design

Zero External Dependencies: 100% synthesized in real time via the browser's native Web Audio API (no external .mp3 or .wav files).
Dual-Mode Ambient BGM:
Crisis Ambient: Deep, melancholic noir ambient chord pads with warm low-pass filtering ().
Harmonic Ambient: Meditative golden sine/triangle ambient pads () celebrating planetary recovery.
Cavernous Climate Blasts: Multi-tap echo delay network ( delay line with low-pass dampening loop) paired with pink-noise shockwaves and sub-bass seismic rumbles.
Master Sound Toggle: Located directly to the left of the RESET button. Supports instant mute/unmute with smooth volume ducking.

📱 Cross-Platform Architecture
Engineered as a single, self-contained .html file with full zero-install support across:
iOS / Safari: Includes viewport-fit=cover and CSS safe-area padding (env(safe-area-inset-bottom)) so flippers never conflict with the iPhone Home Indicator swipe bar. Accommodates WebKit's unique 'interrupted' audio lifecycle with auto-wake on visibilitychange.
Android / Chrome: Full touch-action defense, eliminating double-tap zoom delay or touch-highlight gray boxes.
Desktop Browsers: Hardware-accelerated Canvas with high-DPI scaling protection (Math.min(window.devicePixelRatio, 2)).
